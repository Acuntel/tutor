## Shape of the week

- **Monday and Wednesday are the long days.** 11:00 am to 5:50 pm with two gaps: 12:00 to
  2:00, and 3:00 to 4:00.
- **Tuesday is the heavy single-block day.** Discussion at noon, then a 3 hour 50 minute
  CTCS lecture and screening from 2:00 to 5:50. Nothing before noon.
- **Thursday is nearly free.** One 50 minute discussion at 10:00 am, then nothing.
- **Friday ends at 2:50 pm.**

## Consequences worth planning around

- **Thursday is the obvious service-learning day.** WRIT 150 requires 12 volunteer hours
  outside class time. Thursday after 11:00 am is completely open, and Friday after 3:00 pm
  is too. Three or four Thursday afternoons covers the whole requirement.
- **Minami's office hours are Wed and Fri 9:30 to 10:30 am**, which sits directly before
  WRIT 150 at 11:00 on both days. Attending costs nothing but the walk.
- **The Mon/Wed 12:00 to 2:00 gap** is the natural standing work block, and it lines up with
  Writing Center appointments in THH 216.
- **CTCS Tuesday runs to 5:50 pm.** Anything due Tuesday night gets written before 2:00 pm.
- **WRIT 150 deadlines land on Mondays and Fridays** (WP1 Mon, WP2 Fri, WP3a Mon, WP3 Fri,
  portfolio Wed). Monday due dates mean the work happens over the weekend, not the night
  before.
