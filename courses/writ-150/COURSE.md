points in the class, since they are graded out of 3 on effort and timeliness.

## Pass or fail gates

These are not weighted. Missing them fails the course regardless of point total.

1. All three WPs plus WP3a must be turned in. If any essay is missing on the last day of
   class, the course is failed.
2. More than 9 absences fails the course, per university policy.
3. AI found on WP3 fails the course.

## Service learning requirement

Minimum **12 hours** of volunteering, done outside class time, at a partner organization.
Placement is assigned with preference ranking taken into account.

Options:
- **Francisco Homes.** Supporting formerly incarcerated people transitioning back to
  society: writing workshops, event planning, Welcome Home ceremonies.
- **826LA.** Tutoring and mentoring local students on writing, mainly college essays and
  personal statements.
- **Legacy LA.** Tutoring and mentoring local students in various subjects, plus social and
  leadership programming.
- **Alternative.** Must be vetted by the instructor first. Requires in-person interaction
  with community members and must serve the community in some way.

**Penalty:** every 2 hours not completed drops the final grade a half step. Missing all 12
hours would be a 6 half-step drop, which is catastrophic. Track hours in
`service-learning/hours-log.md`.

## Required meetings

- **3 conferences** with the instructor, one per Writing Project. Mandatory. A missed
  conference counts as an absence and also lowers the participation grade.
- **Peer review** for every WP. Rough drafts submitted at peer review sessions count as
  AWAs and must be typed.

Peer review means classmates read the work. Do not write about anything too personal to
share.

## Course character

Argumentative essays built on an original thesis from personal inquiry, sourced from the
thematic readings and from service-learning experience. The instructor states explicitly
that this is not a sociology, anthropology, or political science course. It is a writing
and critical reasoning course framed by community engagement. Self-reflection on personal
power and privilege in relation to social inequity is an expected part of the work.
