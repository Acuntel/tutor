## Late work rules at a glance

**Writing Projects**
- Accepted up to 1 week late.
- Penalty: half a grade per 2 days late.
- Beyond 1 week: not accepted without prior arrangement.
- **Late Paper Pass:** one per semester. A 3-day extension with no penalty. Notification to
  the instructor is required, ideally before the due date but not strictly necessary. Not
  yet used. Save it for the highest-weight paper that goes wrong, which most likely means
  WP2 or WP3, and remember the portfolio takes no late work at all.

**AWAs**
- Turn in any time while the assignment is still open on Brightspace, up to 1 week late.
- Rubric out of 3: 3 = on time and well done, 2 = on time but incomplete OR late but well
  done, 1 = late and incomplete.
- No excused lates. Do not email asking for full credit on a late AWA.
- 2 missing AWAs are allowed with no penalty.
- Ancillary work in service to a WP cannot be completed after that WP is written or turned
  in. Do the AWA before the essay or it is worth nothing.

**Rewrites**
- One rewrite allowed for the whole semester, on any one WP.
- Must first read all comments and write a 2 to 3 paragraph revision plan, or get a verbal
  plan approved in a conference.
- Instructor must approve the plan before the rewrite starts.
- The rewrite grade replaces the original, up or down.
- 2-week window from the date the paper is returned to opt in.
- Not available to anyone found to have used AI or otherwise cheated.
