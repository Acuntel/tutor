## TB test, only if assigned 826LA or Legacy LA

Free JEP-only dates at Engemann Health Center. Two visits, both required:

- [ ] **Monday 9/21, 9:00 am to 3:00 pm.** Test administered.
      Free windows that day: 9:00 to 11:00 am, or 12:00 to 2:00 pm.
- [ ] **Wednesday 9/23, 10:00 am to 2:00 pm.** Injection site read.
      Free windows that day: 10:00 to 11:00 am, or 12:00 to 2:00 pm.

Miss the first date and you pay for your own appointment, and tests can only be administered
Monday through Wednesday. Miss the second and you have to sort out a misread with Engemann.
No other JEP dates will be offered.

- [ ] Upload results to https://uscdornsife.usc.edu/secure/JEP/SecurityDocuments/
- [ ] If 826LA: also email results to volunteerteam@826la.org
- [ ] If Legacy LA: also send results directly to Legacy LA

Note: prior TB vaccination or exposure can cause a false positive on the skin test. Mention
it to the provider if relevant so an alternative test can be used.

## Money

Transportation costs are reimbursable up to $50 per student. Keep receipts.
JEP does not reimburse any medical appointment costs, including TB tests done outside the
free JEP dates.
