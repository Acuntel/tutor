# JEP partnership: how to sign up, and which to rank

Source: `jep-partnership-options-verbatim.md`. Schedule cross-reference from
`../../../semester/fall-2026-schedule.md`. All weekdays in the JEP document were verified
against a 2026 calendar and every one of them checks out.

## The sign-up is one form

Everything else in that document happens after assignment. The sign-up itself:

1. Go to the JEP application.
2. Rank your **top two** partnerships.
3. **Under step three, select "yes"** to indicate you are signing up for a class, and choose
   your professor's last name: **Minami**. Skipping this detaches your preferences from your
   section.
4. Submit.

**Deadline: Friday, August 28, 2026 at 12:00 noon.**

Questions go to Emma Rendon, JEP Program Specialist, erendon@usc.edu.

### Notes on the near-misses

- **Francisco Homes** is the worst structural fit despite the Thursday workshops being
  perfect. Orientation is Tuesday 3:30 to 5:00 during CTCS, the monthly in-person workshops
  are the last Tuesday 5:00 to 6:00, and every Welcome Home Ceremony (9/29, 10/27, 11/24) is
  a Tuesday 3:30 to 4:45. All of it lands inside the CTCS block.
- **826LA** has the heaviest paperwork but the best hours math. Thursday 11:00 to 4:00 fits
  their required three-hour weekday block, and the **Write for Your Future event on Saturday
  October 17, 9:00 to 3:00** is a single 5 to 6 hour session, roughly half the requirement in
  one day. Their own orientation conflicts with MATH, but the async Sunday makeups do not.
- **Water Drop LA** is the cleanest math of all: four Sunday drops at 3 hours each is 12 hours.
- Orientation conflicts are worth one email, not a veto. Most of these are Zoom and the
  organizers deal with class conflicts constantly. Ask before ruling anything out.

## Recommendation

**Rank 826LA first, Water Drop LA second.**

826LA is mentoring high schoolers on college essays and personal statements. That is the
closest thing on the list to work I can actually do well, the Thursday block fits, and the
October 17 Saturday event covers half the hours in one sitting. It also produces the richest
material for the Writing Projects, since the WPs draw on service-learning as evidence and
this partnership is literally about writing and access to higher education.

Water Drop LA as the backup because the Sunday schedule cannot conflict with anything I
have, and it needs no LiveScan or TB test, so a late assignment still leaves time to start.

The cost of ranking 826LA is the clearance load: LiveScan, TB test, Protecting Minors video,
and a separate 826LA application. That work has dated deadlines and is tracked in
`clearance-checklist.md`.
