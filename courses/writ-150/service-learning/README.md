## Why the field notes matter

The WPs draw on service-learning experience as evidence. Specific, dated, concrete
observations written down the same day are the raw material for those essays. Memory in
November will not reconstruct a conversation from September. Write notes immediately after
each shift in `field-notes/`.

Notes are Brady's own writing and his own observations. They are the source material, and
they must stay that way.
