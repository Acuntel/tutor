# Class notes

One file per class meeting, named `YYYY-MM-DD.md`. MWF schedule.

Worth capturing every session:
- Anything she says about an upcoming assignment. Her spoken instructions often carry
  requirements that are not on the printed prompt.
- Dates announced out loud, especially peer review and conference dates.
- What she praises or criticizes in example writing. That is direct evidence of what she
  grades on, and it belongs in `../instructor-profile.md`.
- Any change to a due date or a policy. If it contradicts the syllabus, log it in
  `../../../OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` with both versions.
