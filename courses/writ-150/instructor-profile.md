## What she says she values

- **Process over product.** She opens with "writing as a process" and calls writing
  "recursive". She built the whole AWA structure around invention, drafting, and revision.
  Prewriting has its own grade weight of 20%.
- **Original thesis from personal inquiry.** Every WP is argumentative and must come from
  the student's own question, grounded in the readings or in service-learning.
- **Firsthand experience as evidence.** The service-learning requirement exists so that the
  arguments are informed by actual contact, not only research.
- **Self-reflection on power and privilege.** She names this explicitly as expected work.
- **Authentic voice.** The anti-bias statement talks about cultivating authentic voices and
  questioning who set standardized academic standards. She is not looking for a generic
  polished academic register.
- **Early communication.** Repeated across the syllabus. She says the key to success is
  early communication with the instructor.

## What she penalizes

- Late AWAs, with no excuses accepted and an explicit "do not email me about it".
- Doing prewriting after the essay, which earns nothing.
- Phones, laptops used off-task, sleeping, and showing up unprepared.
- Not self-reporting a tardy after attendance is taken.
- AI, severely and on an escalating scale.

## Practical read

The highest-leverage moves in this class, given how she has weighted it:

1. **Never miss an AWA.** 20% of the grade, graded on being on time and complete, and two
   freebies exist on top of that. This is the cheapest A in the class.
2. **Show up.** Absences hit the course grade directly after the third, and 10 fails.
3. **Log service hours as they happen.** Each missing 2 hours is a half-step off the final
   grade, and reconstructing hours in December is how people lose them.
4. **Use conferences well.** They are mandatory anyway, they are the sanctioned way to get
   direct guidance on a draft, and a verbal revision plan approved in conference is one of
   the two paths to a rewrite.
5. **Use the Writing Center.** It is free, it is explicitly permitted, and it covers exactly
   the help AI is banned from providing.
